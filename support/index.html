A match looks wrong

The app suggests the closest matches its bundled model can find and shows a confidence figure. It can be wrong, and it is more likely to be wrong on a blurred photo, an unusual angle, or a species outside the guide it ships with. Photograph the subject filling more of the frame, in even light, and compare the clues shown for each candidate before accepting one.

The species I photographed is not in the guide

The app ships a written guide covering 1,500 species. The model recognises considerably more than that, so a correct match may still have no written entry behind it yet.

Access and purchases

The app includes 3 days of full access. After that a single one-time purchase unlocks it permanently; there is no subscription. If you have purchased and the app does not recognise it, use Restore Purchases on the paywall screen. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by the developer.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. Nothing you photograph or save leaves your device.

Butterfly Discover — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

The short version

Butterfly Discover has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no developer server. Identification runs entirely on this device using a model bundled inside the app. Your photos and your observation log stay on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Photos you choose or capture, the species matches shown for them, anything you save to your observation log, and your settings are written to storage on your device only. Deleting the app removes them. There is no backup to a developer server because no developer server exists.

Camera and photo library

Camera access lets you photograph butterflies and moths so the app can suggest a match. Photo library access lets you pick an existing photo for the same purpose. The app does not scan, index, or upload your photo library.

Network access

The app's own code makes no network requests, and identification never requires a connection. On Android, the packaged app declares internet and related permissions because it includes Google Play's billing and asset-delivery components. Those are used by the store, not by the app's identification features.

Purchases

Butterfly Discover is free to download with 3 days of full access. After that, a single optional one-time purchase unlocks the app permanently. There is no subscription and nothing auto-renews. Apple or Google handles purchase and restore processing; Butterfly Discover does not receive payment-card or billing details.

Children

The app collects nothing, so there is nothing collected from anyone of any age.

What this app is for

Butterfly Discover is an identification aid for learning. Its matches can be wrong. It is not a foraging, medical, or safety guide.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
